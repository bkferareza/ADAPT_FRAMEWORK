# Glossary

## Status
ACTIVE

## ADAPT
Governed AI-assisted delivery framework for controlled project execution.

## Source Truth
The accepted durable business or project truth used to guide delivery.

## SOURCE_TRUTH_V0.1
The initial promoted business source document for Paggawa, derived from `Paggawa.docx`.

## Director Lane
The no-code control lane responsible for project movement, intake, assignment, gaps, blockers, decisions, planning approval, and consolidation.

## Workcell
A human-owned execution or validation lane created through onboarding.

## Handoff
An atomic transfer of authority, context, and required output from one lane or workcell to another.

## Context Pack
The smallest safe bundle of context needed for one handoff or execution.

## Context Delta
A record of what changed after an execution.

## Gap
Missing, conflicting, ambiguous, or unproven truth.

## Blocker
A stop condition that prevents lawful progress.

## Evidence
Proof that supports or refutes a claim.

## QA Signoff
Independent validation result from a QA lane or approved QA workcell.

## Developer Self-Validation
Checks performed by the implementing workcell. This is useful evidence but not QA signoff.

## Paggawa Mobile
Mobile-facing surface for residents and workers.

## Paggawa Quest
Barangay-assisted dashboard or tablet-friendly surface for assisted access.

## Resident / Client
A person who needs skilled manual work.

## Worker
A skilled blue-collar service provider seeking local visibility and nearby work.

## Barangay Staff
Assisted-access operator who can create job requests, register workers, view boards, update simple statuses, and record notes.

## Referrer
A community member who can recommend a worker. This is a future or optional trust layer in early scope.

## Match
A record created only when a resident accepts a worker response.
