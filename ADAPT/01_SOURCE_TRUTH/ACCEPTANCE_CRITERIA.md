## Evidence Expectations
Future validation must provide evidence for:
- required user workflow behavior
- privacy-before-match behavior
- job status transitions
- match creation rules
- review and reputation updates
- connected Mobile and Quest state
- absence of prohibited guarantee/certification language

## QA Boundary
These criteria are not QA signoff. They define what future QA workcells must validate independently.
