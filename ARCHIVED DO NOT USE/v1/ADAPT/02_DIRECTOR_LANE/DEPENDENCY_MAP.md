# Dependency Map

## Business Sequence

1. Actor and privacy rules govern all discovery.
2. A resident or barangay-assisted operator creates a job request.
3. Workers discover safe job details and respond.
4. A resident accepts one response, creating a match.
5. Coordination details unlock only for the matched parties.
6. A matched job can be completed once.
7. Completion enables review and updates simple reputation.
8. Mobile and Quest must observe the same job, response, match, completion, and reputation state.

## Decision Dependencies

- Platform, framework, persistence, authentication, and deployment remain undecided.
- Workcell onboarding depends on known people and approved roles.
- Milestone planning depends on an approved first milestone objective.
