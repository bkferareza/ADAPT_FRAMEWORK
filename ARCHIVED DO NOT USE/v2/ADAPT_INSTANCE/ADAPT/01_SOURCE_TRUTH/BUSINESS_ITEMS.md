## Project

Paggawa: Local Skills Discovery and Barangay-Assisted Job Matching Platform.

## Domain Summary

Paggawa makes local skilled labor visible through a connected mobile experience and a barangay-assisted dashboard. It structures discovery and limited trust signals without guaranteeing workmanship or certifying skill.

## Primary Users

- Resident / Client
- Worker
- Barangay Staff
- Referrer, deferred as a future trust-layer role

## Core Workflow

Resident need -> job request -> worker discovery or worker response -> resident acceptance -> match -> completed work -> review and reputation signal.

## Product Surfaces

- Paggawa Mobile for residents and workers.
- Paggawa Quest for barangay-assisted access on tablet, laptop, kiosk, or desktop.

## Core Business Rules

- Exact address, phone number, and direct contact details stay hidden before match.
- A match exists only after the resident accepts a worker response.
- Only matched jobs can be completed, and completion cannot be duplicated.
- Barangay registration or identity checking does not guarantee work quality.
- Trust signals must remain factual and avoid certification claims.
- Barangay staff do not handle payments, force matches, certify skills, or resolve disputes in the MVP.
- Reputation remains simple: completed jobs, ratings, reviews, referrals, and unresolved-complaint indication.

## MVP Non-Goals

Payments, escrow, full chat, real-time messaging, GPS tracking, maps, native mobile apps, production authentication, production database, official LGU integration, legal certification, complex disputes, moderation and appeals, advanced AI matching, ranking algorithms, and nationwide deployment logic.
