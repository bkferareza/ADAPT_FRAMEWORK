## Owned Scope

Changed-file list, command or artifact evidence, source-truth references, gaps, blockers, and handoff status.

## Stop Conditions

Stop for business feature implementation, production authentication, database persistence, Azure resource creation, secrets, or any path outside the approved shell scope.
