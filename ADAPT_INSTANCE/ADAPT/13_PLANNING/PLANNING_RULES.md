# Planning Rules

Planning reads accepted source truth, milestone objective, workcell registry, capacity, lane assignments, dependencies, blockers, gaps, and carry-over.

Planning recommends bounded slices, dependency sequence, workcell roadmaps, QA timing, integration timing, and overextension risks. Director approves. Planning may not approve itself, assign work to nonexistent workcells, finalize technology, or mutate code.
