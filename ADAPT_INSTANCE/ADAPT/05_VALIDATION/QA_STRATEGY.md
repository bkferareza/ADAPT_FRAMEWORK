# QA Strategy

## Initial Validation Concerns

- Exact address and contact details never appear before match.
- Non-matched workers cannot access coordination details.
- Barangay public-style views do not expose resident private information.
- One accepted response creates one match.
- Only matched jobs complete, and completion cannot be duplicated.
- Review is allowed only after completion and updates simple reputation.
- Mobile and Quest observe consistent records and statuses.
- Trust labels do not imply guaranteed quality or legal certification.
- Barangay permissions and internal notes remain appropriately restricted.

Independent QA strategy, environments, tooling, and automation remain open under GAP-007.
